About these records

Customer IDs are anonymized. Late delivery is counted only for delivered orders whose recorded delivery calendar date is later than the estimated date. Undelivered orders are not counted as late deliveries in this historical sample.

The data shown is a small excerpt, not a representative statistical sample of all Olist orders.
